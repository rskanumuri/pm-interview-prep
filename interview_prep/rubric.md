# Interview Practice Scoring Rubric
## Master Rubric

This is the FALLBACK rubric: company-specific rubrics in `interview_prep/rubrics/` and `sources/<company>/rubric.md` take precedence (see `interview_prep/reference/debrief_core.md` §1). Each interview format has its own evaluation dimensions plus two universal dimensions (Delivery, Company Fit).

---

## Universal Dimensions (All Interviews)

### Delivery (1-5)
| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Reading/reciting, monotone, frequent filler ("um", "like", "so basically"), major time overrun |
| 2 | Stilted but understandable, noticeable filler, some rambling, over time limit |
| 3 | Conversational but unpolished, occasional filler, hits time target ±10 sec |
| 4 | Natural and confident, minimal filler, clean pacing, within time target |
| 5 | Sounds like a senior leader telling their story: crisp, rhythmic, lands the punchline |

### Company Fit (1-5)
| Score | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Generic answer, no connection to the target company or role |
| 2 | Mentions company name but no real alignment to their values/challenges |
| 3 | References company context but doesn't weave it into the answer naturally |
| 4 | Clear connection between your experience and their specific needs/culture |
| 5 | Answer feels like it was built FOR this company: language, priorities, and framing all match |

---

## Company-Specific Rubrics

Format-specific dimensions for a company live in `interview_prep/rubrics/{company_key}.md`. `/company-prep` creates that file and `/process-sources` adds to it. When no company rubric exists, `/pm-practice` uses its built-in base dimensions for the question type and `/debrief` labels the score "unweighted, generic".

---

## Scoring Quick Reference

During practice, each response gets:
- **2 universal scores** (Delivery + Company Fit)
- **3-4 format-specific scores** (varies by interview type)
- **Overall** = weighted average (format-specific dimensions count 2x, universal count 1x)

### Rating Scale
- **1** = Needs fundamental rework
- **2** = Below bar: major gaps
- **3** = At bar: passable but not differentiated
- **4** = Above bar: strong, would advance
- **5** = Exceptional: memorable, senior-leader caliber

---

## Pre-Interview Rules (Built-In Penalties)

Each violation is an automatic -1 (the rules themselves live in CLAUDE.md "Pre-Interview Rules" and `interview_prep/reference/skills_core.md` §6):

1. **Lead with business impact, not technology.** Penalty if you open with architecture/tech before the business problem.
2. **Never introduce a story with what you lack.** Penalty if you say "I don't have X experience, but..."
3. **Never frame a current or recent role as a consolation prize.** Penalty if that role sounds like a fallback.
4. **"Can AI make it faster?" test.** Penalty if a generic AI answer could replace yours. Be specific.
5. **End with offer.** Penalty if you close without what you would bring to this role or a closing question.

---

## Interpretation

| Range | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 4.5-5.0 | Interview ready | Maintain, light reps only |
| 3.5-4.4 | Strong, minor polish needed | Targeted reps on weak dimensions |
| 2.5-3.4 | At bar but not differentiated | Re-read source material, then 3+ reps |
| 1.5-2.4 | Below bar | Rework the answer structure, then re-record |
| 1.0-1.4 | Fundamental gap | Flag for review: may need a new story or approach |
